# Brand Voice — The Daily Roast

## In one line

A small-batch roaster in Bangalore that treats coffee seriously and customers
casually. We sound like a knowledgeable friend behind the bar, not a textbook and
not a hype account.

## Tone dials

| Dial | Where we sit |
| --- | --- |
| Formal ↔ casual | 70% casual |
| Technical ↔ plain | Plain first, technical detail available if you want it |
| Playful ↔ earnest | Earnest about sourcing and freshness, light everywhere else |
| Salesy ↔ restrained | Restrained. We describe, we don't oversell |

## Do

- Lead with taste and everyday use ("this is the one you make without thinking at 7am").
- Name the farm, region, altitude and process when we have them — specifics build trust.
- Use metric and Indian brewing context (South Indian filter, 250g bags, monsoon-season cold brew).
- Write short. One idea per sentence.
- Be honest about trade-offs (a light roast needs a bit more care; dark roast hides origin character).

## Don't

- No "artisanal", "curated journey", "liquid gold", "third wave" as a badge.
- Don't promise health benefits or make claims about caffeine we can't back.
- Don't invent tasting notes, prices, or stock status — if Maya wouldn't say it, we don't print it.
- No fake scarcity. Limited lots are genuinely limited; say the real number.
- Don't talk down to filter-coffee drinkers or instant-coffee drinkers.

## Vocabulary

- "Coffee" not "beans" in headlines; "beans" is fine in body copy.
- "Roasted to order" / "roasted this week" — always tie freshness to a date.
- "Grind" options by brew method, not by number.
- "Maya" is our AI barista — lowercase "barista", always "asks before changing your cart".
- Prices shown in ₹, bags in grams (100 / 250 / 500 / 1000).

## Boilerplate (≤ 50 words)

> The Daily Roast is a small-batch coffee roaster in Bangalore. We buy from named
> estates in India and around the world, roast in small batches to order, and ship
> within days of roasting. There's a subscription if you want it and an AI barista,
> Maya, if you want a hand choosing.

## One-liners for ads / meta descriptions

- "Named-estate coffee, roasted this week in Bangalore, at your door in days."
- "Build a box, subscribe, or just ask Maya what to try first."
- "Roast meters on every coffee, so you know what you're getting before you buy."
