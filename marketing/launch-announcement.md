## Short press blurb (~80 words)

The Daily Roast has opened its own roastery in Bangalore. The move brings roasting
in-house for the first time, cutting the gap between roast day and doorstep to a
few days across India. The opening line-up includes single-estate Indian coffees
from Chikmagalur and the Araku Valley alongside seasonal imports from Ethiopia,
Colombia and Guatemala. Every coffee ships with roast-date labelling, tasting
notes and a roast meter. A subscription and an AI barista, Maya, round out the
storefront.
