Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

The Daily Grind Roastery ("we", "us") operates this storefront at this domain. This policy explains what information we collect when you shop with us and how it's used.

What We Collect

  • Order & shipping details — name, email, shipping address, and phone number (if provided) when you place an order, used to fulfil and ship it.
  • Cart & session data — your cart contents and a randomly generated session identifier are stored in your browser's local storage so your cart persists between visits. This is not synced to any account unless you complete a purchase.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number; Stripe handles that directly under its own privacy policy.
  • Order lookup — order status can be looked up by order number without logging in; treat your order number as you would any reference number tied to your purchase.

How We Use It

  • To process, roast, pack, and ship your order, including sharing your shipping address with our courier partner (Shiprocket) for delivery.
  • To respond to support questions or order issues you raise with us.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to remember your cart, currency preference, and a session identifier. Clearing your browser storage will reset these.

Your Choices

To request a copy of the order or account information we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it, contact us using the details below. We retain order records as required for accounting, tax, and dispute purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions, reach us via the contact details on our storefront.

This policy describes our current data practices in plain terms and is not a substitute for formal legal advice. If you operate this store commercially, have it reviewed by a qualified professional for your jurisdiction before relying on it.
