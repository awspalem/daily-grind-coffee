There's a chat button on every page of the shop. Behind it is Maya — an AI
barista who knows our current menu, your order history (if you're signed in), and
how we brew things. Here's how to use her well.

## What Maya is good at

- **Recommendations.** "I usually drink Bru with milk, what should I try?" or
  "something like the Ethiopian I had last time but with more body." She'll
  narrow the menu to two or three and say why.
- **Brewing help.** Ratios, grind, why your pour-over tastes sour, what to do
  with a French press.
- **Order and subscription questions.** "Where's my order?", "when's my next
  delivery?", "can I swap next month's coffee?"
- **Building a cart by chat.** "Add a 500g bag of Dawn Patrol, ground for filter."

You can type to her or press-to-talk, and she can read her answers back.

## What Maya won't do

We built her with hard limits on purpose:

- **She won't change your cart without asking.** Every add, remove or swap gets
  confirmed by you first.
- **She won't invent an order status or a price.** If she doesn't actually have
  the information, she says so rather than guessing.
- **She won't make health claims** or push you toward the most expensive bag.

If Maya isn't sure, she'll tell you and point you to a human.

## Getting a better recommendation

The more you tell her, the better she does:

- **Name a reference point.** A coffee or brand you already like or dislike.
- **Say how you brew.** Filter, espresso, French press, South Indian filter,
  cold brew — it changes the answer a lot.
- **Say who it's for.** "A gift for someone who says they don't like coffee"
  gets you our sweetest, most forgiving lots.
- **Mention milk.** Black vs. with milk is one of the biggest splits on the menu.

## Why an AI barista at all

A good shop assistant asks a couple of questions and saves you from buying the
wrong thing. Most websites can't do that. Maya can, at 11pm, without making you
read ten product pages first. And the same catalogue she reads is
[open to other AI assistants](https://dailyroast.in/.well-known/mcp.json) too, so
an agent can shop here on your behalf.
