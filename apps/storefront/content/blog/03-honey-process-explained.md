A coffee "bean" is the seed of a fruit — the coffee cherry. Between picking the
cherry and roasting the seed, someone has to get the fruit off. *How* they do
that changes the cup as much as the farm does.

## Washed

The fruit is pulped off within hours, then the seeds are fermented in water to
remove the sticky layer (mucilage) and dried clean.

**Result:** clarity. You taste the coffee itself — the variety, the soil, the
altitude — with little interference. Bright, clean, transparent.

**On our menu:** Colombia Huila Pink Bourbon, Guatemala Antigua. Also the base
of Dawn Patrol.

## Natural (dry process)

The whole cherry is dried in the sun for weeks with the fruit still on. The seed
absorbs sugars and ferments gently inside the fruit.

**Result:** big fruit. Berry, tropical fruit, wine, a heavier body and lower
acidity than a washed version of the same coffee.

**On our menu:** Ethiopia Yirgacheffe Gedeb — jasmine and ripe peach come from
that 21-day dry.

## Honey

The middle path. The skin is pulped off but some or all of the sticky mucilage is
left on the seed to dry. "Honey" refers to that sticky layer, not the taste
(though the taste often does lean honeyed).

**Result:** the sweetness and body of a natural with more of the clean structure
of a washed. Jaggery, stone fruit, syrupy texture.

**On our menu:** Chikmagalur Attikan Estate Honey and Araku Valley Red Honey —
both Indian, both honey-processed, and a great side-by-side lesson in how much
else besides process matters. Attikan is jaggery and red apple; Araku is
jackfruit and wild honey.

## Anaerobic

A newer method: the cherries ferment in sealed, oxygen-free tanks before drying.
Tightly controlled, often intense and unusual.

**On our menu:** Sumatra Kerinci Anaerobic Natural — spiced rum, black cherry,
dark cocoa. Nothing like traditional Sumatran coffee.

## Why it's on the label

Process is the fastest way to predict whether you'll like a coffee. Prefer clean
and bright? Look for washed. Want jammy and sweet? Natural or honey. Want a
conversation piece? Anaerobic.
